# Phase 2 — Lesson 6: Team Meetings & Clarifying Ideas

**Thời lượng:** 120 phút

## 1. Core Vocabulary

`agenda`, `topic`, `clarify`, `confirm`, `agree`, `disagree`, `point`, `context`, `assumption`, `decision`, `action item`, `follow up`, `discuss`.

## 2. Meeting Patterns

### Asking

> Could you clarify what you mean by...?

> Could you give us more context?

> Can you explain why we need this?

> Do we agree that...?

### Confirming

> Just to confirm, we are going to...

> If I understand correctly,...

> So the decision is to...

### Agreeing

> I agree with that approach.

> That makes sense to me.

### Disagreeing politely

> I see your point, but I have a concern.

> I'm not sure that approach will scale well.

> I would consider another option.

### Following up

> I'll take care of that.

> I'll follow up with the backend team.

> Let's discuss this separately.

## 3. Reading — Technical Meeting

> The team is discussing how to improve the performance of a slow API. One developer suggests adding a cache, while another suggests optimizing the database query first.
>
> The team discusses the trade-offs of both approaches. They agree that query optimization should be investigated first because it does not introduce additional infrastructure.
>
> The team decides to measure the current query performance before making a change. The backend developer will investigate the slow queries and share the results in the next meeting.

## 4. Translation

1. Could you give us more context?
2. Just to confirm, we are going to optimize the query first.
3. I see your point, but I have a concern.
4. That makes sense to me.
5. I'm not sure this approach will scale well.
6. Let's discuss this separately.
7. I'll follow up with the backend team.
8. The decision is to investigate the database query first.

## 5. Reverse Translation

1. Bạn có thể cho chúng tôi thêm context không?
2. Để xác nhận lại, chúng ta sẽ tối ưu query trước.
3. Tôi hiểu ý bạn, nhưng tôi có một lo ngại.
4. Điều đó hợp lý với tôi.
5. Tôi không chắc cách tiếp cận này sẽ scale tốt.
6. Chúng ta hãy thảo luận vấn đề này riêng.
7. Tôi sẽ follow up với backend team.

## 6. Practical Meeting

Viết một đoạn hội thoại 10 lượt giữa:

- Developer A
- Developer B

Chủ đề: API đang chậm.

Phải có:
- đề xuất
- đồng ý
- lo ngại
- hỏi clarification
- decision

**80/20:** `Could you clarify...?` / `Just to confirm...` / `I see your point, but...` / `That makes sense.` / `I'll follow up...`
